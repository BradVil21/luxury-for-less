Section 01

Overview & Who We Are

Welcome to Luxury For Less Car Service, a personal driver and car service business owned and operated by Al Kaufman, based in Fort Lauderdale, Florida and serving all of Broward County, FL.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the personal information you provide when you visit our website at www.luxuryforlesscarservice.com, contact us to book a ride, or otherwise interact with our car service business.

By using our website or submitting your information through our booking form, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not submit your information through our website.

Short version: We are a small, local Fort Lauderdale personal driver service. We only collect the information you give us when making a booking inquiry, and we use it solely to serve you - nothing more.

Section 02

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide to us when you reach out to book a personal driver in Fort Lauderdale or Broward County, FL. We do not collect information passively beyond standard analytics tools described below.

Information You Provide Directly

When you fill out the booking inquiry form on our website, we collect the following:

  • First & Last Name - so we know who we're serving
  • Phone Number - so Al can call or text to confirm your booking
  • Email Address - for booking confirmation and follow-up communications
  • Type of Service - e.g., airport transfer, medical appointment, personal driver for the day
  • Message / Trip Details - pickup location, destination, dates, and any special requests

Information We Collect Automatically

When you visit our website, standard web technologies may automatically collect certain information including your general geographic location (city/region level), browser type, device type, pages visited, and referring website. This data is collected through Google Analytics and Google Ads tracking as described in the Cookies section below.

Payment Information

We do not collect or store payment information through our website. Payments are handled directly between you and Al Kaufman via cash, credit card, Venmo, or Zelle at the time of service.

Section 03

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide exclusively to operate our personal driver and car service business in Fort Lauderdale and Broward County. Specifically, your information is used to:

  • Respond to your booking inquiry and confirm your ride with Al Kaufman
  • Contact you by phone, text, or email to coordinate pickup times, locations, and trip details
  • Send booking confirmations or scheduling reminders relevant to your car service
  • Improve our website and service experience based on how visitors use our site
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations in the State of Florida

We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing, automated profiling, or any purpose unrelated to fulfilling your car service request in the Fort Lauderdale and Broward County area.

Section 04

Information Sharing & Disclosure

Luxury For Less Car Service is a small, owner-operated business. We take your privacy seriously and do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

Make.com operates under its own privacy and security standards. You can review their practices at make.com/en/privacy-notice.

Google Services

Our website uses Google Analytics and Google Ads (Google Tag Manager with Ads ID AW-18068003831) to measure website traffic and the effectiveness of our advertising in the Fort Lauderdale and Broward County market. Google may collect anonymized usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Legal Disclosure

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental authority, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety, or investigate fraud.

We will never sell your personal information. Your name, phone number, and email are only ever used to confirm and complete your car service booking in Fort Lauderdale or Broward County.

Section 05

Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to understand how visitors interact with our site and to improve the performance of our Google Ads campaigns targeting Fort Lauderdale and Broward County residents.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. They help us recognize returning visitors, understand page performance, and measure the effectiveness of our advertising - for example, whether someone searching for a "personal driver in Fort Lauderdale" visited our site after clicking one of our ads.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics) - Track aggregate website usage to help us understand visitor behavior and improve our site experience
  • Advertising Cookies (Google Ads) - Help us measure the performance of our paid search campaigns and remarketing efforts in the South Florida market
  • Essential Cookies - Necessary for the basic functioning of our website

Your Cookie Choices

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. Please note that disabling cookies may affect certain features of our website. You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking by using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Section 06

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected - namely, to complete your car service booking and maintain reasonable business records.

Booking inquiry records are generally retained for up to 24 months from the date of inquiry for the purpose of service history and customer follow-up. After this period, records are deleted or anonymized unless retention is required by applicable Florida or federal law.

You may request deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting Al directly as described in the Contact section below.

Section 07

Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Our website transmits form data using HTTPS (SSL encryption), and our third-party service providers - including Make.com and Google - maintain their own industry-standard security practices.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security. We encourage you to contact us by phone at (702) 784-4600 if you have concerns about the security of your information.

Section 08

Your Rights & Choices

As a resident of Florida or anywhere in the United States, you have the following rights regarding your personal information held by Luxury For Less Car Service:

  • Right to Access - You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Right to Correction - You may request that we correct any inaccurate personal information
  • Right to Deletion - You may request that we delete your personal information from our records, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Right to Opt Out of Communications - You may ask us to stop contacting you at any time by calling or texting (702) 784-4600

To exercise any of these rights, please contact Al Kaufman directly by phone, text, or email using the contact information in Section 11. We will respond to all requests within a reasonable timeframe.

If you believe that a child under 13 has submitted personal information to us through our website, please contact us immediately at (702) 784-4600 and we will promptly delete the information.
